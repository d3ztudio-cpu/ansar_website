# Staff exam instant results — rules review

Scope: `staffExamSessions/{examId}/answers/{answerId}` and `questions/{questionId}`.

The only post-submission client reads are permitted after the authenticated
participant's own participant document has reached `submitted` or `scored`.
During `waiting` and `active` states, answer keys remain unavailable. Questions
remain readable while active, and become readable again only after the attempt
is locked so the participant can review their answers. Answer and question
documents contain no PII. Clients never gain write access to answer keys,
scores, or other participants.

Attack review: unauthenticated users fail authentication; a different account
fails because the status lookup uses its own UID; active attempts fail the
status check; submitted attempts can read keys but cannot update their locked
answer set or score fields; answer-key writes remain controller-only.
